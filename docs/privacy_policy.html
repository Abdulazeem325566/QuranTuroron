Privacy Policy — Muslim Kids

Last updated: October 2026

Information We Collect

Muslim Kids does not collect, store, or share any personal information. The app works fully offline except for advertisements. No account, no login, no tracking by us.

Advertising (AdMob)

This app uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may collect and use device identifiers and usage data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads, as described in Google's Privacy Policy. You can opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads → "Opt out of Ads Personalization").

Children's Privacy

This app is designed for children and families. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. AdMob is configured to treat this app's audience appropriately and does not serve personalized ads to child-directed traffic where required by policy.

Text-to-Speech

The "listen" feature uses your device's built-in text-to-speech engine. No audio or text is sent to our servers (we have none).
