Muslim Kids 🌙 — Islamic learning made simple and fun for children.

🤲 DAILY DUAS (16)
Essential duas every Muslim child should know — with Arabic text, Urdu translation, and a listen button to hear the pronunciation.

📿 SIX KALIMAS
All six Kalimas with Arabic, Urdu meaning, and audio.

✨ 99 NAMES OF ALLAH
Asma-ul-Husna with simple Urdu meanings — learn who Allah is, one beautiful name at a time.

🕌 LEARN NAMAZ
Step-by-step guide: the 5 daily prayers, wudu (ablution) steps, and how to pray — perfect for kids learning Salah for the first time.

🔢 TASBEEH COUNTER
Digital dhikr counter with targets of 33, 100, 500, and 1000 — tap and track your progress.

WHY PARENTS LOVE IT
• Simple, clean design made for kids
• No account needed — open and learn
• Works offline for reading (audio needs device speech)
• Free forever

Made with love for Muslim families everywhere. 🤍
